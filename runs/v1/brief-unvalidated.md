# SRVCABLE — Research brief

As of 2026-09-23 · Supplied-document research

## Snapshot

- Q1 FY27 revenue grew 18% to ₹1,248 crore; profit rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore. Use the official figures: S1’s ₹1,428 crore and 35% growth conflict with the filing. [S3][S3]
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17%; this is a forecast, not a guaranteed outcome. [S2][S2]

## Bull case

- A larger order book supports demand visibility, although delivery timing and cash collection remain important. [S3][S3]
- Bharuch could expand capacity materially, while data-centre cables offer another growth opportunity if management’s expectations hold. [S3][S3] [S2][S2]
- Promoter pledging has fallen substantially, reducing this particular shareholder risk compared with last year. [S8][S8]

## Bear case

- Margins weakened despite revenue growth. Contract resets leave earnings exposed to a delay in recovering higher raw-material costs. [S3][S3] [S2][S2]
- Slower utility payments increase working-capital pressure, although management reports no defaults. [S2][S2]
- A ₹46.3 crore GST demand creates uncertainty. Management intends to appeal and expects no material impact; that assessment does not establish the outcome. [S7][S7]

## Open questions

- Will copper cost recovery restore margins within management’s stated timeframe? [S3][S3]
- Will Bharuch start on schedule, and can collections support remaining spending without materially increasing debt? [S2][S2]
