# SRVCABLE — Research brief

As of 2026-09-23 · Supplied-document research

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors; its MD is Anil Deshpande. [S3][S3]
- Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year over year. [S3][S3]
- Quarterly profit after tax rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore. [S3][S3]

## Bull case

- The larger order book supports revenue visibility; conversion remains uncertain. [S3][S3]
- Management expects Bharuch to add approximately 30% capacity, with commercial production scheduled for Q3 FY27. [S3][S3]

## Bear case

- EBITDA margin fell 170 basis points; management says contractual price resets lag costs. [S3][S3] [S2][S2]
- CFO Meera Iyer reported slower collections, increasing working-capital pressure. [S2][S2]
- A ₹46.3 crore GST demand creates exposure; the company's favorable legal assessment and intended appeal do not establish the outcome. [S7][S7]

## Open questions

- What explains the news revenue discrepancy? The original company filing takes precedence; no reconciliation is supplied. [S1][S1] [S3][S3]
- Has promoter pledging changed since June? Prefer the newer exchange filing's 4.1%; the March 2024 report's 35% is historical, not current. [S8][S8] [S4][S4]
