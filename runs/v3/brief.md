# SRVCABLE — Research brief

As of 2026-09-23 · Supplied-document research

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd, listed as SRVCABLE, manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. Its latest supplied results cover the quarter ended 30 June 2026. [S3][S3]
- Consolidated quarterly revenue rose 18.0% to ₹1,248 crore; profit after tax rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore. Revenue growth alone does not establish volume growth. [S3][S3]
- Operating earnings, measured by EBITDA, increased only 2.6% to ₹140 crore. The margin fell from 12.9% to 11.2%, showing weaker profitability per rupee of revenue. [S3][S3]
- At June 2026, promoters held 58.2%; pledged shares were 4.1% of promoter holding, down from 11.5% a year earlier. Pledging remains an ownership risk despite that reduction. [S8][S8] [S4][S4]

## Bull case

- Orders support the growth case: the June order book reached ₹3,900 crore versus ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. Exports contributed 21% of revenue versus 16%. [S3][S3]
- Management expects Bharuch to add about 30% cable capacity and start commercial production in Q3 FY27. It maintains 15–17% FY27 revenue growth guidance; these are expectations, not completed execution. [S3][S3] [S2][S2]

## Bear case

- Copper cost recovery takes time: management says about two-thirds of contracts allow price adjustments, with a one-to-two-quarter lag. This leaves near-term margin recovery uncertain. [S2][S2]
- Collection slowed as receivable days rose from 78 to 96. Management reports no defaults, but slower utility payments coincide with ₹620 crore Bharuch spending, 70% completed, funded mainly internally plus term debt. [S2][S2]
- The latest adverse disclosure reports a ₹46.3 crore GST demand including interest and penalty. Management intends to appeal; its expectation of no material impact does not establish the eventual financial cost. [S7][S7]

## Open questions

- Why does reporting show ₹1,428 crore and 35% growth? Prefer the original quarterly release’s ₹1,248 crore and 18.0%; the packet does not explain the discrepancy. [S1][S1] [S3][S3]
- Has the GST appeal been filed, and what payment or provision is required? The disclosure establishes an intention, without confirming filing or a successful outcome. [S7][S7]
- What is valuation at the as-of date? The supplied ₹1,012 price is historical; current pricing, share count and full-year earnings evidence are missing. [S1][S1]
