# SRVCABLE — Research brief

As of 2026-09-23 · Supplied-document research

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors and identifies its NSE ticker as SRVCABLE. This distinguishes it from the similarly named local television and broadband operator. [S3][S3] [S6][S6]
- For Q1 FY27, consolidated revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0%, while net profit rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore. Profit growth lagged revenue growth. [S3][S3]
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17%. This is an expectation; the packet supplies no quantified volume growth, so revenue growth alone cannot establish higher physical sales. [S2][S2] [S3][S3]
- The latest supplied ownership filing, dated to June 2026, shows promoters holding 58.2%. Pledged shares were 4.1% of promoter holding, down from 11.5% in June 2025; exposure remains. [S8][S8]

## Bull case

- The June order book grew to ₹3,900 crore from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. Exports reached 21% of revenue versus 16%, supporting a broader mix of demand. [S3][S3]
- Bharuch could add about 30% to cable capacity, with production planned for Q3 FY27. Management also expects data-centre cables, currently about 9% of domestic revenue, to double that share within two years. [S3][S3] [S2][S2]

## Bear case

- Operating earnings margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9%. Management attributes pressure mostly to copper; price adjustments cover about two-thirds of contracts but lag one to two quarters. [S3][S3] [S2][S2]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96, tying up cash as utility payments slowed. Bharuch's ₹620 crore investment was about 70% spent, funded mainly internally plus term debt; completion still needs funding. [S2][S2]
- September's GST demand totals ₹46.3 crore including interest and penalty. Management intends to appeal and expects no material impact, but neither a filed appeal nor a successful outcome is established. [S7][S7]

## Open questions

- Why does reporting give revenue of ₹1,428 crore and 35% growth? Prefer the original company results' ₹1,248 crore and 18.0%; the discrepancy remains unexplained. [S1][S1] [S3][S3]
- Has the GST appeal been filed, and what payment or provision will be required? The disclosure establishes only an intention to appeal. [S7][S7]
- What is the as-of valuation? The packet gives an older ₹1,012 share price, but lacks current pricing, share count and sufficient earnings data for a valuation assessment. [S1][S1]
